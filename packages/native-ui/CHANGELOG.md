# Changelog

## 0.5.0

Pieces the Verve, vibot and Velth phone apps need, from their API specs (the app sources were not available here). New components, optional props on existing ones, and `Text` on `/primitives`. Every existing prop keeps working. No new dependency or peer; the lockfile is unchanged.

- **`Text` on `@vvedantb/native-ui/primitives`.** `Text` and `TextClassContext` from `src/primitives/text.tsx`, the cva text with the `h1`–`h4`, `p`, `lead`, `large`, `small`, `muted`, `code` and `blockquote` variants (semibold or medium face with its weight as a style). Not on the root entry: no other `src/primitives` part is there, and a root `Text` would sit beside React Native's. Its colours are NativeWind classes (`text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground`), unlike the components, which use styles; where an app defines `foreground` with a nested `dark` key, pass `style={{ color }}` in dark mode.
- **New `ColorSwatches`** (root and `/primitives`): `value`, `onChange(color)`, `colors` (`#rgb` or `#rrggbb`), `size` (`sm` 24, `md` 32, `lg` 40; a step up with larger text), `labels`, `accessibilityLabel`, `disabled`, `testID`.
  - A wrapping row (`gap-3`) of round swatches. The selected one, matched without case, has a 2 ring in the foreground colour, with a 2 gap, and a check in black or white, whichever has the higher WCAG contrast with it (`swatchCheckColor`; no check for a colour that is not hex). Each swatch has a faint outline (foreground at 12%), so white shows on a white surface.
  - A radio group: each swatch is a `radio` with `checked` and `disabled` state, named by `labels[i]` or its colour string. Pressing calls `onChange` with a selection haptic. With the ring a `md` swatch is 40 across, and its hit slop brings it to 44; the 12 gap keeps neighbouring slops apart. `disabled` dims the row to 50%.
- **`DateTimeField`** (root and `/date-time`), backwards compatible. New `DateTimeFieldProps` type.
  - `mode="date"`: the date button only. `datetime` (default) and `time` are unchanged.
  - `value` may be `null`: each button then shows `placeholder` (default "Select date" or "Select time") in `palette.fieldPlaceholder`. Opening the picker with no value starts it on today, or on `maximumDate` when that is earlier (`pickerSeed`); picking a date calls `onChange`. On iOS the spinner sets the value when it is turned.
  - `onClear`: while there is a value, a 44 round button with a muted cross follows the buttons. It closes the picker and calls `onClear`, not `onChange`. Named "Clear date" ("Clear time" in `time` mode), or `clearLabel`.
- **`ListRow`**, backwards compatible. New `ListRowProps` type.
  - `leading`: any node at the start, such as an `Avatar` with an unread dot, shown in place of `icon`. `icon` still works without it.
  - `titleEnd`: a node at the end of the title line, such as a `Badge` or a time. The title takes the rest of the line and is cut off first.
  - `control`: a node at the end of the row, outside the pressable area. The row is then a view holding the pressable body (`flex-1`, with `testID`) and the control beside it, so pressing a `Switch` there does not call `onPress`. `right` stays inside the pressable area. Without `control` the row is one element, as before.
  - `onLongPress`: on the pressable. A row with only `onLongPress` is pressable too.
  - `accessibilityLabel`: names the pressable row; default `title`. Use it when `leading` or `titleEnd` carries meaning, for example "Ada, unread, 09:41".
- **New `StatGrid`** (root and `/primitives`): `items` (`label`, `value`, `testID`) in rows of `columns` (2, default, or 3). Each tile is the flat web card (`cardStyle`: `surfaceCard`, radius 16, no shadow) with the web `Surface` padding (`p-4`): the label in muted `text-sm`, then the value in semibold `text-xl` with tabular figures (`text-base` and `text-2xl` with larger text). The last row is padded with empty cells, so every tile has the same width (`statGridRows`). VoiceOver and TalkBack read a tile as one element: "Streak, 12 days". No items, no grid.
- **New `SectionLabel`** (root, `/layout` and `/primitives`): the quiet label above a group of rows ("Today", "Library"). `text-xs` medium (12 / 500), muted, with slightly open letter spacing (0.02em); `text-base` with larger text. `uppercase` sets capitals with the web menu label tracking (`tracking-widest`, 0.1em). A header for assistive tech. No side padding: pass `className="px-4"` to line it up with `ListRow` text. The web `sidebarSectionLabelClass` is 11 px, -0.01em and the muted colour at 55%; this label uses the full muted colour, as other muted text in the package. On the light page background (`#f5f5f5`) that is 4.4:1, where the 55% colour would be 2.1:1 (computed WCAG ratios; 7.8:1 and 3.0:1 in dark).
- **`Card` `onPress`.** With `onPress` the card sits in a `Pressable` button that fades to 70% while pressed, named by `title` or the new `accessibilityLabel`; `testID` moves to the button. Without it the card is unchanged. A pressable card is one element for assistive tech, so do not put other controls in it. New `CardProps` type.
- **New `SheetRow`** (root and `/primitives`): one action in a `Sheet` or bottom sheet. `icon` (Tabler), `title`, `detail`, `active`, `danger`, `disabled`, `onPress`, `testID`.
  - A touch size of the web menu item: at least 56 high (64 with larger text), `px-4 py-3`, a 22 icon (26), a `text-base` medium title (`text-xl`) and a muted `text-sm` detail. While pressed it takes the web menu highlight (`bg-default`, `palette.default`) at radius 12.
  - `danger` draws the title and icon in `palette.danger` (3.6:1 on the white sheet, 4.0:1 on the dark one: under 4.5:1, as the web danger text); `active` adds a check at the end and reports `selected`; `disabled` dims the row to 50%, as the web item, and ignores presses. A button named by `title`, with `detail` as the hint.
- **New `PageIndicator`** (root and `/primitives`): `count`, `index` (from 0), `onChange`, `testID`. The web `CarouselDots`: dots 6 high, the current one 16 wide in `palette.primary` and the others 6 wide in the muted colour at 30%, 6 apart (8, 20 and 8 with larger text). Nothing for a single page, as on the web. Without `onChange` it is one element that reads "Page 2 of 5". With it each dot is a button named "Page 2 of 5", `selected` on the current page, in a 44 square: the dots then sit 44 apart, so each has a whole target with no overlapping slop.
- **`StatusDot` `info` tone**: Tailwind `blue-500` (`#3b82f6`) in light and `blue-400` (`#60a5fa`) in dark. The web `StatusTone` has no `info`, and `tokens.css` has no `--info`, so the colour is local to `StatusDot`, not a `palette` key (the palette test converts `tokens.css`).
- **New `StackPageHeader`** (root, `/layout` and `/page-title`): `title`, `description`, `onBack`, `backLabel` (default "Back"), `headerRight`, `backTestID`, `safeTop` (default `true`), `testID`, `className`.
  - A stack screen header with no fill, for screens over `AmbientBackground`. A back `HeaderIconButton` with a Tabler `IconChevronLeft` at the start (none without `onBack`), `headerRight` at the end, and a centred `PageTitle` (Instrument Sans 600 at 20 / 25, -0.5 tracking) with an optional muted description of up to two lines.
  - Both sides grow equally and are at least as wide as the wider of the back button (48, or 56 with larger text) and `headerRight` (measured with `onLayout`), so the title stays centred on the screen and a long one is cut off before it reaches them.
  - Like `PageHeader`: the `px-4` screen padding, the top safe-area inset (`safeTop={false}` under a native header) and a row at least 56 high below it.
  - On `/page-title` it brings the Tabler package (a required peer) to that entry for the chevron: Metro does not tree-shake by default. The entry still reaches no optional peer or loader.
- **New `IconButton`** (root and `/primitives`): `icon`, `label`, `onPress`, `disabled`, `variant` (`default` or `overlay`), `size` (`sm` 32, `md` 40, `lg` 48; 44, 48 and 56 with larger text), `testID`, `iconColor`.
  - `default` is a round bordered surface (`tokens` surface, border and foreground), the look of `HeaderIconButton` in style colours. `overlay` is a black circle at 35% with a 20% white hairline and a white icon, the same in light and dark, for camera chrome over a live preview.
  - A button named by `label`, with `disabled` state; it reaches 44 with its hit slop. A public component of its own; the AI chat `ChatIconButton` is internal to `/ai` and unchanged.
- **New `CaptureButton`** (root and `/primitives`): a camera shutter. A 76 ring (4 wide, white) around a 60 white disc, over a black tint at 15%, in both schemes. The disc shrinks to 90% while pressed (not when `disabled`, which dims it to 50%). A button named "Take photo" by default (`accessibilityLabel`). Larger than 44, so no hit slop.
- **Dark mode needs Tailwind `darkMode: "class"`.** In the Expo web gallery under `?scheme=dark` the page was dark but `DateTimeField`, `ListRow`, the `StatGrid` tiles and cards were light. The gallery app had no Tailwind `darkMode`, so it was `media`, and NativeWind's `setColorScheme` threw ("Unable to manually set color scheme without using darkMode: class"). The app caught the error and painted only its own page from the URL, so `useScheme` stayed light. Any app without `darkMode: "class"` has the same fault, on web and native. It is also why the 0.4.0 AI composer and code block, which read `useScheme`, can look white in an app forced dark. The README (Expo / NativeWind, Theme, `ThemeToggle`) now says to set `darkMode: "class"` for `ThemeToggle` and any `setColorScheme` call.
- **`ThemeToggle` and a throwing `setColorScheme`.** The press catches the error, logs the `darkMode: "class"` fix with `console.error` and leaves the scheme as it is, instead of throwing from the press handler. With `darkMode: "class"` it works as before.
- **Fills as styles.** Even with the right scheme, a NativeWind fill class stays light in dark mode where an app theme nests `dark` under the key (the `text-foreground` fault fixed in 0.3.3). These fills are now styles:
  - `ListRow`: `tokens.color.surfaceSecondary`, or `surface` with `onCanvas`, not `bg-surface-secondary` and `bg-surface`.
  - `DateTimeField` buttons: `palette.default` (the web `bg-secondary`), not `bg-secondary`.
  - `HeaderIconButton`, and so `ThemeToggle` and the `StackPageHeader` back button: `tokens.color.surface` with the white hairline (40% in light, 10% in dark), not `bg-surface` and `dark:border-white/10`.
  - The other new 0.5.0 components already used styles. Not changed here, and still on class fills: the `EmptyState` card, `Chip`, the `Toast` action, `UpgradeToProCard` and the `src/primitives` parts.
- **Instrument Sans on Expo web.** The serif type in the gallery came from the gallery app, not the package: it mapped the `font-instrument-sans-*` classes to `System` and loaded no font, so the browser drew its default serif. The package is unchanged; apps load the font and map the classes. The README now shows the Expo web set-up: the `@vvedantb/ui` woff2 files served from `public/fonts/`, and every class mapped to `"Instrument Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`, with the weight from the style the components set.
- **Gallery.** `ParityGallery` adds a pressable `Card`, `info` in the `StatusDot` row, and sections after EmptyState: ColorSwatches (enabled and disabled), DateTimeField (date only with placeholder and clear, and date and time), ListRow (avatar with an unread dot, `titleEnd` badge, long press, and a `Switch` control), SectionLabel and StatGrid, SheetRow (on the overlay surface: plain, detail, sort choices with `active`, disabled and danger), PageIndicator (read-only and pressable), StackPageHeader (on `AmbientBackground` strips, with a long title), and Camera chrome (overlay buttons and the shutter on a dark stand-in preview, and the `default` sizes). Names and figures there are placeholders.

- `docs/native-ui-parity.md`: the `ListRow`, `Card`, `StatusDot` and `DateTimeField` notes, and a list of the new native-only pieces. The summary counts are unchanged, as they count web exports.

## 0.4.0

AI chat parts on a new `@vvedantb/native-ui/ai` entry, ported from the `@vvedantb/ui` AI elements for phones. They are generic: no model names and no app logic. The wording is "the AI", "Thinking…", "Thought for 12s" and "Worked for 12s".

- **New entry: `@vvedantb/native-ui/ai`** (`src/entries/ai.ts`, re-exporting `src/ai/index.ts`).
  - Not on the root entry. Metro does not tree-shake by default, so on the root the parts would bundle into every app, with or without a chat. The root exports are unchanged.
  - It reaches no optional peer, not even a loader. It uses `react-native-reanimated` (shimmer, fade-ins) and `react-native-safe-area-context` (composer), both required peers.
- **Thread: `Conversation`, `ConversationContent`, `ConversationEmptyState`, `ConversationScrollButton`, `useConversation`.**
  - `ConversationContent` is a `ScrollView` (web `gap-4 px-3 py-3`) that sticks to the bottom. While the reader is within 70 of the end (`AT_BOTTOM_THRESHOLD`, `isScrolledToBottom`), new and growing content stays in view: a jump, not an animation, as streamed text grows the content many times a second. Once the reader drags up, it stops following.
  - `ConversationScrollButton` shows while away from the end: a round outline `Button` (`icon`, 40, slop to 48) with `IconArrowDown`, centred 16 above the bottom (web `absolute bottom-4`). It has an opaque surface fill, as it sits over messages, and no shadow. Pressing it scrolls down (animated, or a jump with Reduce Motion) and hides it at once; its own scroll on the way down does not bring it back. It fades in and out through `NativeOnlyAnimatedView`. Label "Scroll to bottom".
  - `ConversationEmptyState`: the web title (medium, a header) and muted description, with an optional Tabler icon.
  - The scroll view keeps taps for the keyboard (`keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled"`) and dismisses it on drag (interactive on iOS).
- **Messages: `Message`, `MessageContent`, `MessageActionsBar`, `MessageAction`.**
  - `Message` (`from`: `user`, `assistant` or `system`) is the web `w-full max-w-[95%] gap-2`; a user message sits at the end of the row.
  - A user `MessageContent` is the web bubble: `palette.primary` fill, `palette.primaryForeground` text, `px-4 py-3`, radius 16 (`rounded-2xl`) with the bottom-right corner at 14 (`rounded-br-md`, `tokens.radius.md`). `userBubbleStyle(scheme)` exports the style. An assistant message has no fill and the foreground colour.
  - `MessageContent` draws a string child as `ResponseText`, and hands its text colour to the `ResponseText` inside it (`ChatTextColorContext`): native text does not inherit a colour from its parent.
  - `MessageAction` is a ghost icon button at the web `icon-sm` size (32, round, 16 icon, slop to 44; 44 with a 20 icon with larger text). `kind` gives the icon and label: `copy` ("Copy"), `retry` ("Retry"), `like` ("Good response"), `dislike` ("Bad response") (`MESSAGE_ACTIONS`). `icon` and `label` override it. `selected` draws the icon in the foreground and reports `selected`, for feedback. No tooltips.
- **Reasoning: `Reasoning`, `ReasoningTrigger`, `ReasoningContent`, `useReasoning`, `reasoningMessage`.**
  - As on the web it opens when streaming starts (unless `defaultOpen={false}`), times the stream (whole seconds, rounded up), and closes once, one second after it stops. `open` / `onOpenChange` control it; `duration` sets the time for a reloaded thread.
  - Differences from the web, as in `useStreamingDisclosure`: a reader can close it while it streams (the web reopens it), and a toggle after the stream cancels the pending close.
  - The trigger is a brain icon, the shimmering "Thinking…", then "Thought for 12s" ("Thought for a few seconds" with no duration; the web says "Thought for 12 seconds"), and a chevron that turns up when open. Its accessibility label is the message, with `expanded` state. `getThinkingMessage` returns a string.
  - The content is muted monospace text on the web `rounded-lg bg-surface-secondary/40 p-3` panel. Plain text, not Streamdown.
- **Trace: `AgentTrace`, `AgentTraceLine`, `AgentTraceIcon`, `AgentTraceTitle`, `AgentTraceDescription`, `AgentTraceCollapsible`, `AgentTraceCollapsibleTrigger`, `AgentTraceCollapsibleContent`.**
  - The web parts and statuses: `pending` fades a line to 60%, `error` turns it `palette.danger` (`traceLineColor`). The line hands its colour to the icon, title and description through context.
  - `AgentTraceTitle shimmer` pulses a running step. `AgentTraceDescription` is faded (70%) with tabular figures.
  - The collapsible trigger has a right chevron that turns down when open. `trailing` controls (a pressable `FileChip`) sit outside the trigger, as on the web; the chevron toggles too and is hidden from assistive tech. The content is indented behind a rail under the icon centre (web `ml-[calc(--spacing(2)-1px)] border-l pl-4`).
  - Spread `useStreamingDisclosure(isStreaming)` on `AgentTraceCollapsible` to open it while a step streams. No `iconKey` crossfade.
- **`ActivityStep`**: one tool or activity step as a trace line (icon, label, status, description). `running` pulses the label and shows a 16 `Spinner`, `complete` a check, `error` a warning in the danger colour. Its accessibility label reads, for example, "Read 3 files, done, Worked for 12s", with `busy` while running. A light stand-in for the web `ActivityTasks`.
- **`ChainOfThought`**, simplified: header (brain, "Chain of Thought", chevron), content, steps (`complete`, `active`, `pending`) with a rail, and search-result chips (`secondary` `Badge`, regular `text-xs`). No `ChainOfThoughtImage` or step animation.
- **Composer: `PromptInput`, `PromptInputButton`, `PromptInputSubmit`, `isGenerating`, `ChatStatus`.**
  - The web field surface (`fieldStyle`: `rounded-field border bg-field-background`), with a `header` slot (attachments), a multiline field and a footer with a `toolbar` slot and the send button. Placeholder "What would you like to know?", as on the web; accessibility label "Message".
  - The field starts at one line and grows to 160 high (about 6 lines; 240 with larger text), then scrolls. It is the `Textarea` field (the `Input` primitive, field text colour, placeholder colour, larger-text face and weight) without `Textarea`'s `numberOfLines` (default 4), which on Android sets the line count.
  - Send is an up arrow on the primary fill, at the web `icon-sm` size with a 44 target; with nothing to send it is `secondary` and disabled. While `status` is `submitted` or `streaming` it is a filled square labelled "Stop" that calls `onStop` (disabled without one). `onSubmit` gets the trimmed text, then the field clears. `value` / `onChangeText` control the text.
  - It sits in a `KeyboardAvoidingView` (`behavior="padding"`, on iOS and Android, as the dialogs) and pads the safe-area insets. The keyboard covers the home indicator, so the bottom inset is taken off the lift and the composer sits on the keyboard. `keyboardVerticalOffset` is for a navigation header above the screen; `safeBottom={false}` for a tab bar below.
- **`Suggestions`, `Suggestion`**: a horizontal `ScrollView` of round chips with no scroll bar (a phone swipes; the web scroll arrows are not ported), or `orientation="vertical"`. A chip is the web `rounded-full border bg-surface-secondary px-3 py-1.5 text-sm text-muted-foreground`, and takes the web hover fill and colour while pressed. 32 high with a 44 target. `onPress` gets the suggestion text.
- **`Sources`, `SourcesTrigger`, `SourcesContent`, `Source`**: a fold of link rows. "Used 1 source" / "Used 3 sources" (`sourcesLabel`). A `Source` row (`accessibilityRole="link"`) opens its address with `Linking.openURL`, or hands it to `onPress`.
- **`FileChip`**: the web `rounded-md border bg-secondary px-1.5 py-0.5 font-mono text-xs` chip, cut in the middle so the extension shows. A labelled button with `path` and `onOpenFile`, plain text otherwise.
- **`Attachments`, `Attachment`, `getMediaCategory`, `getAttachmentLabel`**: `inline` (wrapping) or `list` chips with a media icon (or a 20 thumbnail for an image with a `url`), the name and a remove button labelled "Remove" and the name (20, slop to 44). `AttachmentData` holds the `FileUIPart` / `SourceDocumentUIPart` fields the web reads, without the `ai` SDK. No hover cards or `grid` previews.
- **`CodeBlock`, `CodeBlockCopyButton`**: plain monospace code (`MONO_FONT`: Menlo on iOS, `monospace` elsewhere), selectable, scrolling sideways, on the web `flatPanelSubtle` fill (`bg-surface-secondary`, no border or shadow) at radius 16. A header shows the language and, with `onCopy`, a copy button: it calls `onCopy(code)`, shows a check labelled "Copied" for 2 s (as the web), ignores presses meanwhile, and keeps the copy icon if `onCopy` rejects. No shiki, line numbers or language selector.
- **`ResponseText`**: the markdown stub. Plain, selectable text with newlines kept, 16 (`text-xl` with larger text). There is no markdown peer and none is added. Apps that want markdown can add one of these and render it in place of `ResponseText`: `react-native-markdown-display`, `@ronradtke/react-native-markdown-display` (a maintained fork) or `react-native-marked`. The stub's comment lists them too.
- **Clipboard.** React Native's own `Clipboard` is deprecated (moved out of core) and is not declared in `types/peers.d.ts`, and no clipboard package is added (`expo-clipboard` is not used). `MessageAction kind="copy"` calls the app's `onPress`, and `CodeBlock` the app's `onCopy`.
- **Shared parts.**
  - `Collapsible`, `CollapsibleTrigger`, `CollapsibleContent`, `CollapsibleChevron`, `useCollapsible`: a small fold, as native-ui has no `Collapsible`. The trigger is a `Pressable` button with `expanded` state and a 44 target from its 28 row. The content mounts only while open and fades in over 150 ms through `NativeOnlyAnimatedView` (in place with Reduce Motion). The chevron turns at once (the web eases it over 150 ms).
  - `Shimmer`: the web gradient sweep cannot clip to glyphs on native without a masked view, so the foreground text fades to 0.45 (`SHIMMER_LOW_OPACITY`) and back once per `duration` (seconds, default 2), on the UI thread with Reanimated. With Reduce Motion it holds still in the muted colour.
  - `useStreamingDisclosure`: the web hook, unchanged.
  - `formatElapsed(seconds)`: "12s", "1m", "1m 5s". `chatTextSize(kind, largerText)`: `body` 16 (web `text-sm`, 14; the phone body size), `small` 14 (triggers, trace lines, sources; the web sources trigger is 12) and `caption` 12, each a step up with larger text.
- **Phones and accessibility.**
  - Touch targets reach 44 with `hitSlop` (`touchSlop`) or their size: fold triggers (28 rows), icon buttons (32), suggestion chips (32), source rows (36), file chips (22) and remove buttons (20). The scroll button reaches 48.
  - Roles and states: buttons and links have roles and names; folds report `expanded`, feedback `selected`, send `disabled`, a running step `busy`. Line icons are hidden from assistive tech.
  - Type and icons scale with `useLargerText`, and font scaling is never turned off. Colours come from `palette` and `tokens` as styles (`textStyle`, `fieldStyle`, `buttonColors`), in light and dark, with no NativeWind colour class. No shadows.
- **Gallery.**
  - New `@vvedantb/native-ui/ai-gallery` exports `AiChatGallery`, a full chat screen, and `AiChatSamples`, its thread. The thread has a user message; a finished assistant message with folded reasoning and trace, a reply, code, sources and actions; a second user message; and an answer that streams with its reasoning open until Stop is pressed. The screen adds the scroll button, suggestions, and the composer with sample attachments and an attach tool. The entry imports `/ai`, not the root, so it reaches no optional peer.

## 0.3.1

- **Android glass blur.** With `expo-blur` installed, `FloatingSurface` now blurs on Android as well as iOS. So do `FloatingTabBar`, `Card`, `SettingsSection`, toasts, the `Select` popover and `GlassSheetBackground`. Before, Android always drew the solid fill. The solid fill is now used only when `expo-blur` is not installed, with `blur={false}`, or when Reduce Transparency is on. Web keeps the CSS backdrop blur.
- Android `BlurView` settings: `experimentalBlurMethod="dimezisBlurView"`, plus `blurMethod="dimezisBlurView"` (the name since expo-blur 55), `blurReductionFactor={3}`, and the iOS `tint` (`systemMaterialLight` / `systemMaterialDark`) and `intensity={70}`. The translucent glass fill is laid over the Android blur, because the iOS blur samples that fill and dimezis does not. A factor of 3 blurs harder than the expo-blur default of 4 and keeps the radius (70 / 3, about 23) within the 25 px limit of the RenderScript blur that Dimezis uses below API 29. These values are derived from the expo-blur 58.0.5 source (its Android tint table and radius formula). They have not been checked on a device.
- `GlassBlurProvider` and `GlassBlurTarget` now work; before, they only passed their children through. `GlassBlurTarget` renders an expo-blur `BlurTargetView` (a plain `View` without `expo-blur`), takes `View` props and an `active` prop, and becomes the Android blur target for floating surfaces in the same `GlassBlurProvider`. Since expo-blur 55, Android dimezis blur needs a target. Without one, expo-blur logs a warning and draws only its tint. **Layout change:** `GlassBlurTarget` now adds a view with `flex: 1`. Pass `style` if that does not suit.
- **Optional peers.** `expo-blur` and `expo-haptics` are now optional peers. The package loads them with a `require` inside `try`, which Metro treats as an optional dependency, so `@vvedantb/native-ui` imports and runs without them. Without `expo-haptics`, `selectionHaptic()` does nothing. With it, `FloatingTabBar`, `Tabs` and `Checkbox` presses still fire the selection haptic on iOS and Android.
- New entries: `@vvedantb/native-ui/blur` (`loadBlur`, `glassMode`) and `@vvedantb/native-ui/haptics` (`loadHaptics`, `selectionHaptic`). The `.`, `./gallery` and `./package.json` entries are unchanged.
- Known: from expo-blur 55, each Android `BlurView` logs a deprecation warning for `experimentalBlurMethod` when it mounts. A surface with no target also logs a "no `blurTarget`" warning.
- `types/peers.d.ts`: `BlurView` takes `experimentalBlurMethod`, `blurMethod`, `blurReductionFactor` and `blurTarget`, and `BlurTargetView` and `require` are declared. No new dependencies; the lockfile is unchanged.
