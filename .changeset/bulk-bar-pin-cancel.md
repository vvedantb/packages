`BulkActionBar` takes `pinCancel`. With it, Cancel selection (X) and its divider sit outside the sideways scroll, at the bar's right end, so the X shows even when the bar is wider than a phone screen. The count, Select all and the actions still scroll. Default `false`: the X scrolls with the actions, as before and as the web.
