Add `Sidebar.RailGroup`: a shared trough for rail tiles, styled like segmented tabs. Tiles inside a group take the `bg-segment` pill when active and a half-strength pill on hover. `railTileStateClass` takes an optional `grouped` flag, and `railGroupClass` and `useInRailGroup` are exported for custom tiles. The account trigger follows the group fills too.
