`ActivityQuestionCard` (ai entry): the read-only record of an answered AskUserQuestion prompt, as the web `ActivityQuestionCard` — each question with its topic badge, lettered options with the picked ones checked on the primary mark, free "Other" text with a pencil, and "Answered in the next message" when no structured answer was kept. `selectedLabels` and `freeTextAnswer` read an answer as the web does. `ChoiceQuestion`'s topic badge is shared with it.
