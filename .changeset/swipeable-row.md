New `SwipeableRow` (on the root and `/primitives` entries), with `closeSwipeRows`, `swipeActionsAccessibility` and the `SwipeAction` and `SwipeableRowProps` types. A horizontal swipe moves the row aside to reveal its `leadingActions` (swipe right) or `trailingActions` (swipe left): icon buttons on the `default`, `warning` or `danger` fill, the first at the screen edge. The row follows the finger 1:1 and, on release, springs on at the finger's speed: past half its buttons it stays open, else it closes. A full swipe (half the row, with the buttons already in view) runs the first action: a light haptic and a slightly larger icon as it arms, and an action with `exits` slides the row out (a rejected promise from its `onPress` brings it back); other actions spring the row back. A fling counts toward each decision; a fling back cancels. Toward a side with no actions, and past the row's width, it rubber-bands. The pan needs 12 pt sideways before it takes the row, and fails after 8 pt vertical, so a small diagonal drag scrolls the list. One row is open at a time: another row opening, a tap on any row (it only closes) or `closeSwipeRows` (pass it to a list's `onScrollBeginDrag`) closes it. With Reduce Motion on it still follows the finger, and the release settles at once.

The swipe runs on Gesture Handler worklets, on the UI thread, and needs `GestureHandlerProvider` from `@vvedantb/native-ui/gesture-handler`; without it the row is drawn as is, with no swipe, and no Gesture Handler import. The buttons behind the row are hidden from screen readers: spread `swipeActionsAccessibility(actions)` on the row's main pressable, so VoiceOver and TalkBack offer the same actions by label.
