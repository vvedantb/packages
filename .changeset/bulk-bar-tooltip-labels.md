`BulkActionBar` gains `labels`: `"inline"` (default, icon plus label, label hidden below `sm`) or `"tooltip"` (icon-only at every width, label in a tooltip, skip-confirm hint as a corner badge). Set it per bar or app-wide with the `UiConfig` entry `BulkActionBar: { labels }`. New types `BulkActionBarConfig` and `BulkBarLabels`.
